Privacy Policy

Hydration Station  ·  Last updated May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dylan Matuschka ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Hydration Station mobile application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described here. Questions? Email us at WIMSBY1@icloud.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and display name. This data is stored securely via Supabase and is used solely to provide the service.

Hydration logs. Your daily water-intake entries, beverage types, notes, and goals are stored on-device and, if you are signed in, synced to our secure cloud database so you can access your data across devices.

Apple Health data. If you grant permission, the App reads and writes water-consumption records to Apple HealthKit. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We use it only to show you integrated health insights within the App and to keep your HealthKit record up to date.

Push notification token. If you enable reminders, we store your device's push-notification token to deliver hydration reminders. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchase information. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We receive anonymised entitlement status (e.g., whether your subscription is active) but never your payment card details.

Usage data. We do not use any third-party analytics SDK. Basic crash and error information may be captured to help us fix bugs; this data does not include personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the App and its features.
  • To sync your hydration data across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To send you push notifications you have opted into (hydration reminders, daily summaries).
  • To verify your subscription status and unlock Pro features.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Hydration Station accesses Apple HealthKit to read and write water-intake data. Health data is processed entirely on your device and is never shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party not listed in this policy. We will not use HealthKit data for advertising or for any purpose not disclosed here.

5. Data Retention

Your account data and hydration logs are retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at WIMSBY1@icloud.com. We will action deletion requests within 30 days. Local HealthKit records are managed through the Apple Health app on your device.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption for data in transit and encrypted storage at rest via Supabase. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.

  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at WIMSBY1@icloud.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of sale (not applicable here). To submit a request, email WIMSBY1@icloud.com.
