Privacy Policy

Hydro Hero  ·  Last updated May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dylan Matuschka ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Hydro Hero mobile application (the "App"). Hydro Hero is designed to be local-first: there is no Hydro Hero account, no Hydro Hero server, and most of your data never leaves your device. By using the App you agree to the practices described here. Questions? Email us at WIMSBY1@icloud.com.

1. Information We Collect

Hydration logs. Your daily water-intake entries, beverage types, goals, streaks, and badges are stored entirely on your device using iOS local storage. There is no Hydro Hero server that holds a copy of your data, and no cloud sync. If you delete the app, all of this data is gone.

Apple Health data. If you grant permission, the App reads and writes water-consumption records to Apple HealthKit. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers (we have no servers). We use it only to keep your HealthKit record up to date with what you log in the App.

Squad feature. If you use the Squad feature, you generate a short "progress code" containing your chosen username and summary stats (hydration percent for the day, streak, etc.). You share this code manually (via Messages, email, AirDrop, QR code, etc.) with people you choose. Hydro Hero does not transmit Squad data to any server; codes pass directly through whatever channel you choose.

Notifications. If you enable hydration reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. We do not collect or transmit a push notification token — there is no server that pushes notifications, every reminder is scheduled and delivered locally.

Purchase information. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device identifier (not linked to your identity) and your subscription/purchase status. We never see or receive your payment card details.

Crash and error reports. If the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, diagnostic information (stack trace, device model, iOS version) is sent to Sentry to help us identify and fix bugs. These reports do not include your hydration data or personally identifiable information.

Location. If you grant permission, the App may briefly access your approximate location to check local temperature and suggest extra hydration on hot days. Your location is never stored or shared with any third party.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the App and its features.
  • To deliver locally-scheduled notifications you have opted into (hydration reminders).
  • To verify your subscription status (via RevenueCat) and unlock Pro features.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs (via Sentry).
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Hydro Hero accesses Apple HealthKit to read and write water-intake data. Health data is processed entirely on your device and is never shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party not listed in this policy. We will not use HealthKit data for advertising or for any purpose not disclosed here.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Hydro Hero stores all your data locally on your device, retention is entirely under your control. To delete your data, simply uninstall the App — everything goes with it. HealthKit records remain in Apple Health unless you also delete them via the Apple Health app.

RevenueCat retains your purchase/subscription status for as long as required for accounting and to honor your entitlement. Sentry retains crash reports for 90 days by default.

6. Data Security

Your hydration data is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage protections. The limited data we transmit to RevenueCat and Sentry is sent over TLS. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will address it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability — you can export your hydration history as a CSV from the Pro Settings menu inside the App.

Because Hydro Hero is local-first, most of these rights you can exercise yourself by managing data inside the App. For anything else, email us at WIMSBY1@icloud.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of sale (not applicable here). To submit a request, email WIMSBY1@icloud.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
